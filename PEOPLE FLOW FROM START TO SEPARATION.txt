1. ONBOARDING
Pre-boarding: Administrative setup, background checks, system access requests, and sending welcome packages prior to Day 1.

Orientation: Introduction to company culture, policies, workplace tools, and team members.

Role Alignment: Setting clear initial expectations, 30-60-90 day performance goals, and assigning a buddy or mentor.

2. INTEGRATION
Job Training: Deep-diving into role-specific tools, processes, and workflows.

Early Feedback: Regular 1-on-1 check-ins with managers to address questions, adjust workload, and evaluate fit.

Probation Review: Formal evaluation at the end of the probation period (typically 3 to 6 months) to confirm full-time status.

3. DEVELOPMENT & GROWTH
Performance Management: Continuous feedback, periodic performance reviews (quarterly/annually), and key metric tracking.

Skill Building: On-the-job learning, workshops, certifications, and leadership training.

Career Pathing: Internal promotions, lateral transfers, or succession planning to align employee aspirations with organizational goals.

4. RETENTION & ENGAGEMENT
Total Rewards: Annual salary reviews, performance bonuses, benefits enhancements, and recognition programs.

Workplace Culture: Fostering work-life balance, team building, and open channels for employee feedback (e.g., pulse surveys).

Work Redesign: Adjusting responsibilities or flexible working arrangements to prevent burnout and maintain motivation.

5. SEPARATION / RESIGNATION 
Notice & Resignation: Receipt of formal resignation letter, agreement on the transition timeline, and notification to key stakeholders.

Knowledge Transfer: Handing over ongoing tasks, documentation, project files, and client accounts to team members.

Offboarding & Off-boarding Logistics:

Conducting an Exit Interview to gather candid feedback on company culture and management.

Revoking system access and collecting company property (laptops, badges, keys).

Processing final pay, benefits continuation (COBRA/pension info), and issuing clearance certificates.
