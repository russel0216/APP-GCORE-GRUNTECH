# Payroll and payslips — plan (2026-11-23)

The owner's item 6 ("Payslip add") and his answers: G-CORE COMPUTES the
payslip (reversing "no payroll" in G-FIN's scope — payroll lives in G-HR);
pay basis set per person; SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG and BIR tax computed
from tables kept in HR Settings, which HR can override on a payslip;
overtime, holidays and rest days, night differential and allowances on top;
a payroll run approved by the HR Manager, the Finance Manager and the CEO /
President before the payslips are released.

Nothing is built yet. This is the plan to approve.

## What it reads (already in G-HR)

- **The cutoff**: HR Settings' `cutoffMode` (semi-monthly 1–15 / 16–end, or
  monthly) and `cutoffFor()` in `shared/records.ts` — a run is one cutoff.
- **Days worked, late and undertime**: `Attendance` and the daily time
  record's rule (`dtrDay()`), the same one My Records shows.
- **Paid leave**: approved leave of a type with `LeaveType.isPaid`.
- **Holidays**: Company Settings › Holidays (regular / special).
- **Overtime**: APPROVED `OvertimeRequest` at its `actualHours`.
- **Cash advances**: a loan's repayment by salary deduction already exists
  (`PaymentMethod.SALARY_DEDUCTION`); the payslip would record it.

## What it adds

- **On the employee record** (HR, behind Pay Rates' rights, never shown to
  anyone else): Pay basis — Daily or Monthly; Monthly rate (daily stays);
  allowances (name, amount per cutoff, taxable or not); tax status (already
  there: S, ME …); SSS / PhilHealth / Pag-IBIG / TIN (already there).
- **HR Settings › Payroll**: the SSS bracket table, PhilHealth (rate, floor,
  ceiling), Pag-IBIG (rate, cap), the BIR withholding table per pay period,
  the premiums (regular holiday 200%, special day 130%, rest day 130%,
  rest day on a holiday, night differential 10% from 10 PM to 6 AM), and the
  working days a month for turning a monthly rate into a daily one (26 or
  22 — the owner's call). Seeded with the tables as G-CORE knows them, to be
  checked by HR against the current SSS / PhilHealth / Pag-IBIG / BIR issues
  before the first run.
- **Payroll Runs** (G-HR › People, a new screen): HR raises a run for a
  cutoff; G-CORE computes a payslip per active employee — days, hours, each
  earning and deduction as a line — which HR reviews and may override line
  by line with a reason (kept, audited). Submit sends it through the one
  approval engine (HR Manager › Finance Manager › CEO / President). Approved,
  each payslip is RELEASED: the employee is told and reads it under My
  Records › Payslips; a run can be pulled back to draft until anything is
  paid. Its papers: the payroll register (every person, every column,
  landscape) and each payslip, in the house dress; an Excel of the register
  for the bank file.
- **Who reads what**: the employee their own payslips only; HR the runs;
  Finance and the CEO the run while it waits on them. Pay never appears on
  an audit row's text or any other screen (the pay-rate rule of Phase 2).

## Still to decide before building

1. **Monthly-rated daily equivalent**: 26 days a month (rest day Sunday —
   G-CORE's work week is Mon–Sat) or 22?
2. **13th month pay**: computed by G-CORE in December from the year's basic
   pay, or left out for now?
3. **When the government shares are taken**: SSS, PhilHealth and Pag-IBIG
   split across both semi-monthly cutoffs, or all on the second?
4. **Cash advance deductions**: set per person per cutoff by finance (the
   repayment schedule), or entered by HR on each payslip?
5. **The employer's share**: shown on the payroll register for finance
   (cost of each person), or the employee's share only?

## Size

A large build in about four pushes: (1) the rates, allowances and Payroll
settings; (2) the computation and the run, reviewed and overridden by HR;
(3) the approval, release, the employee's payslip under My Records and the
papers; (4) the register's Excel and the checks in verify-hr.
